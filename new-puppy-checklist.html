Heat matters in South Florida. Walk early morning or evening, watch hot pavement with the back of your hand, and always bring water on outings.

A calm routine beats a perfect schedule

Puppies thrive on predictability. If you keep sleep, food, potty and play roughly the same each day, house training and manners fall into place on their own. You do not need to get everything perfect. You need to be consistent.
