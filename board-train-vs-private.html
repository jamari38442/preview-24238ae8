Board & Train vs. Private Lessons: Which Is Right for Your Dog?

First Option K95 min read

Both programs get you to a well-behaved dog. They just take different paths. Here is a straight comparison so you can pick the one that fits your dog, your schedule and your goals.

Board & train, in short

Your dog stays with us for one to three weeks and trains every day in a structured environment. We do the heavy early lifting, then coach you through the handoff so the results transfer home.

  • Best for: busy owners, fast results, stubborn habits, or building serious off-leash reliability.
  • Trade-off: a bigger upfront investment, and your dog is away for the program.
  • What makes it work: the turnover sessions. A trained dog still needs a trained owner.

Private lessons, in short

Your dog stays home and we train you and your dog together, session by session. You are hands-on from day one and you build the skills at your own pace.

  • Best for: owners who want to be deeply involved, and for steady, week-by-week progress.
  • Trade-off: results depend on your practice between sessions.
  • What makes it work: consistency. Ten focused minutes a day beats one long weekend session.

There is no single right answer. The best program is the one you will actually follow through on. That is exactly what we sort out in a free evaluation.

A simple way to choose

If you need results fast or you are stuck on a specific problem, board & train is often the shortcut. If you have the time and you want to grow your own handling skills from the start, private lessons are a great fit. Many owners do a board & train first, then a few private sessions to keep sharpening.
